Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 30, 2026

AnswerDoctor is an educational prototype that helps teachers and students organize academic work and compare answers. This policy explains the information used to provide the service.

Information we collect

When you create an account, we store your name, email address, role, and account activity. Passwords are stored only as secure hashes. If you choose Google Sign-In, we receive your basic Google profile information: your name, verified email address, profile picture, and Google account identifier.

How information is used

We use this information to authenticate you, maintain your account, save your work, and operate and improve AnswerDoctor. Google Sign-In is used only for authentication. AnswerDoctor does not request access to Gmail, Google Drive, contacts, or other Google services.

Storage and sharing

Account and application data are stored in AnswerDoctor's hosted database and are transmitted to service providers needed to operate the application. We do not sell personal information.

Your choices

You can stop using the service at any time. To request access to or deletion of your account data, email naught.aaru@gmail.com. You may also revoke AnswerDoctor's Google access from your Google Account settings.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated as the prototype develops. Questions can be sent to naught.aaru@gmail.com.
